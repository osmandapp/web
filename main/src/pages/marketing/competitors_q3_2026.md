## Key Competitive Trends — Q3 2026

### 1. Car navigation became the main battleground

**Google Maps**
- Immersive Navigation on Android Auto, lane highlighting, Gemini voice guidance.

**Organic Maps**
- CarPlay dashboard, maneuvers on head-up displays.

**CoMaps**
- Lane guidance, CarPlay improvements, Android Auto fixes.

**Guru Maps**
- CarPlay favorites and commute suggestions.

Free OSM-based apps are closing the gap with Google in in-car navigation — directly in OsmAnd's core space.

---

### 2. Organic Maps and CoMaps keep shipping fast

Organic Maps added:

- Route-stop order optimization
- Better alternative routes
- CarPlay dashboard
- Bulk bookmark/track editing

CoMaps added:

- Lane guidance and maneuver previews
- Track recording during navigation
- Mangrove user reviews

Both remain free and open-source and release roughly monthly.

---

### 3. Smarter route planning and 3D visualization

**Ride with GPS**
- 3D Maps in planning and navigation.

**Komoot**
- Flyover 3D activity videos, rebuilt Android planner.

**Gaia GPS**
- Web route editor upgrade.

**Mapy.com**
- Draw-to-edit routes (Premium).

**Guru Maps**
- Elevation-aware hiking ETA.

Route planning is becoming more visual and more precise — and is often a paid feature.

---

### 4. Offline maps get more flexible

**Komoot**
- Download any custom map area.

**Apple Maps**
- Improved offline map updates.

**MAPS.ME**
- Contour lines and hill shading.

Competitors are moving into OsmAnd's traditional strength: flexible, detailed offline maps.

---

### 5. Community, partnerships and devices drive growth

**Strava**
- Events and Races hubs, adidas partnership, pre-installed on Samsung watches.

**AllTrails**
- 100M members, REI partnership.

**Komoot**
- Global Community Events.

Growth increasingly comes from partnerships and preinstalls, not only from features.

---

### 6. AI in maps goes agentic

**Google Maps**
- Ask Maps books hotels, orders food, uses Gmail/Calendar context.

**Apple Maps**
- AI Flyover, natural-language route search.

AI is still mostly city/POI-oriented; outdoor and offline navigation innovation in Q3 remained concentrated around:

- Car integration
- Alternative routes and multi-stop optimization
- 3D and elevation
- Offline area downloads
- Sensors and wearables

---

### 7. Pricing stayed stable — promotions instead of price changes

No competitor changed list prices in Q3. Pricing activity was promotional:

- Outdooractive Summer Sale (25% off)
- Komoot 30-day Premium trial (La Vuelta)
- Strava free subscription with Samsung watches

# Main Competitors to Watch After Q3 2026

## Organic Maps

**Why:**
- Monthly releases with major navigation features.
- Route-stop optimization and better alternative routes.
- CarPlay dashboard and head-up display support.
- Still free and open-source.

## CoMaps

**Why:**
- Fast catch-up on navigation: lane guidance, maneuver previews.
- CarPlay and Android Auto support.
- Track recording during navigation.

## Komoot

**Why:**
- Custom-area offline downloads.
- Rebuilt route planners on iOS and Android.
- Flyover videos and global Community Events.

## Locus Map

**Why:**
- AR navigation and e-bike sensor support.
- Continues expanding advanced outdoor functionality after Android Auto.

## Google Maps

**Why:**
- Immersive Navigation and lane guidance on Android Auto / CarPlay.
- Agentic AI raises user expectations for search and trip planning.

## Ride with GPS

**Why:**
- 3D maps inside live navigation.
- Strong position with cyclists in North America.
